= Cohort Borrowing and Reclaim
:navtitle: Cohort Borrowing

A *cohort* is a group of ClusterQueues that can share unused quota with each other.
When one queue is full, workloads can *borrow* idle capacity from another queue in the same cohort.
This improves GPU utilization -- without cohorts, idle GPUs in the training pool sit wasted even when inference is overloaded.

In this chapter, you will:

. Configure both ClusterQueues as a cohort
. Fill the T4 inference pool and watch a third workload borrow from the A100 training pool
. Discover that without `reclaimWithinCohort`, the lender *cannot* take its GPU back
. Enable reclaim and verify that the lender can evict the borrowed workload

== How Cohort Borrowing Works

image::cohort-borrowing.svg[Cohort Borrowing Architecture,width=900]

== Key Concepts

* *`cohortName`* -- groups ClusterQueues into a shared pool
* *`lendingLimit`* -- how many GPUs a queue is willing to lend (protects its own capacity)
* *`borrowingLimit`* -- how many GPUs a queue can borrow from the cohort
* *Flavor fungibility* -- the T4 queue lists `a100-gpu` as a second flavor; Kueue tries `t4-gpu` first, then falls back to `a100-gpu` when T4 is full

== What Changes from the Original Configuration

The original `3-cluster-queues.yaml` had two isolated ClusterQueues.
The cohort-enabled `9-cohort-cluster-queues.yaml` adds:

|`spec.cohortName`
|`gpu-cohort`

|--

.Why Not Just Increase the T4 Quota?
[sidebar]
****
You could give the T4 ClusterQueue a quota of 3 GPUs (instead of 2) to handle overflow, but that would permanently reserve an A100 GPU for inference -- even when the training pool needs it.

Cohort borrowing is dynamic: the T4 queue only uses A100 capacity when T4 is full _and_ A100 is idle.
When the training pool needs its GPUs back, reclaim preemption (covered in xref:reclaim-fix.adoc[Enable Reclaim]) returns the borrowed resource automatically.
This maximizes utilization without permanently over-provisioning either pool.
****
