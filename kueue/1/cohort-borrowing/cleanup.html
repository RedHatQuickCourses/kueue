Clean Up and Revert

Clean up all cohort workloads and revert to the original isolated queue configuration.

References

  • Kueue: Cohort

  • Kueue: Fair Sharing (preview of next chapter)

  • Red Hat build of Kueue: Using Cohorts

  • Red Hat build of Kueue: Configuring Fair Sharing

Lab: Clean Up

1. Clean up all cohort workloads

  1. Delete training jobs:

    $ oc delete -f workloads/job-reclaim-preemption.yaml
job.batch "training-reclaim-1" deleted from training-jobs namespace
job.batch "training-reclaim-2" deleted from training-jobs namespace
job.batch "training-reclaim-3" deleted from training-jobs namespace
job.batch "training-reclaim-4" deleted from training-jobs namespace

  2. Delete inference deployments:

    $ oc delete deployment inference-fill-1 inference-fill-2 inference-borrow -n inference-apps
deployment.apps "inference-fill-1" deleted from inference-apps namespace
deployment.apps "inference-fill-2" deleted from inference-apps namespace
deployment.apps "inference-borrow" deleted from inference-apps namespace

  3. Verify everything is cleaned up:

    $ oc get pods -n training-jobs
No resources found in training-jobs namespace.

$ oc get pods -n inference-apps
No resources found in inference-apps namespace.

2. Revert to isolated queues (undo cohort)

  1. Remove cohort borrowing and return to the original isolated queue configuration:

    $ oc apply -f kueue-config/3-cluster-queues.yaml
clusterqueue.kueue.x-k8s.io/a100-cluster-queue configured
clusterqueue.kueue.x-k8s.io/t4-cluster-queue configured

    This overwrites both ClusterQueues with the original definitions — no cohort, no borrowing, no lending.

Summary

You have demonstrated the full cohort borrowing lifecycle:

  1. Cohort setup — joined both ClusterQueues into gpu-cohort with lending and borrowing limits

  2. Borrowing — the T4 pool exhausted its 2 GPUs, and a third workload borrowed 1 GPU from the A100 pool. The borrowed pod landed on the A100 node (visible in nvidia-smi output)

  3. Reclaim failure — without reclaimWithinCohort, the A100 pool could not take back its lent GPU even when its own workloads needed it. The 4th training job stayed pending

  4. Reclaim fix — adding reclaimWithinCohort: Any let the A100 pool evict the borrowed workload and admit its own 4th training job

In the next chapter, you learn how Kueue uses Dominant Resource Share (DRS) to make proportional preemption decisions across teams. In this chapter, reclaim was all-or-nothing — either the lender can reclaim or it cannot. Fair sharing goes further by considering how much each team is consuming relative to its fair share before deciding whether to preempt.
